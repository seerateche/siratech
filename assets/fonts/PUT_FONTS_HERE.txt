Required font files (free, Google Fonts / SIL OFL):

1) Amiri.ttf  -> REQUIRED for Arabic captions burned into video.
   Download: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Amiri
   Then uncomment the `assets:` and `fonts:` blocks in pubspec.yaml.

2) Optional (UI typography):
   Cairo-Regular.ttf / Cairo-Bold.ttf  -> https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cairo
